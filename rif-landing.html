International improv festival in Rome

The international improv festival in Rome

For three days every March, Rome becomes a meeting point for improvisers from all over Europe and beyond: workshops by day with international teachers and live shows at night, all in English.

A chance to train with teachers you rarely meet in Italy, challenge yourself in another language and connect with improvisers from other schools.

Open to every schoolFrom 2nd yearAll in English

Travel info

Coming to Rome

Everything you need to plan the trip, in one place.

The venue

From the airport

Fiumicino (FCO): Leonardo Express train to Roma Termini, about 30 minutes. Ciampino (CIA): shuttle bus to Termini, about 40 minutes.

Where to stay

Look for a place near a metro stop. Write to us and we’ll suggest areas that work well for the venue.

Rome in March

Mild days, roughly 8–17 °C. Bring layers and clothes you can move in.
