Global Improv Festivals: iO Fest 2026 and The Big Sing Musical Improv Festival

Explore two standout improv festivals: Chicago’s iO Fest 2026 and Barcelona’s Big Sing Musical Improv Festival, each showcasing unique training legacies and creative energy.

2026-10-02 · Redazione Artyou Roma

In the sprawling world of improvisational comedy, festivals act as crossroads where years of training and spontaneous creation collide. Two events stand out for their distinct flavors: iO Fest 2026 and The Big Sing Musical Improv Festival.

iO Fest 2026 is the flagship celebration of Chicago’s iO Theater, a venue that has shaped long‑form improv since 1981. The festival appears alongside iO’s regular offerings of weekly classes, house teams, and a five‑level training program that has inspired schools worldwide. While exact dates are not listed in the current schedule, the fest is promoted as a weekend of streaming shows, live performances, and intensive workshops, echoing iO’s commitment to both education and entertainment.

The Big Sing Musical Improv Festival, organized by the Barcelona Improv Group, brings a similar spirit to Spain’s stage. Since 2011 the group has delivered weekly shows, drop‑in sessions, and corporate training, positioning itself as Barcelona’s premier improvised theatre company. The festival adds a musical twist, offering audiences a blend of hilarious, clever, and heartfelt improv that showcases the city’s growing improv community.

Attending a festival lets improvisers step outside their usual venue, encounter diverse styles, and network with peers from different schools. Whether it’s the high‑energy atmosphere of iO Fest or the intimate, music‑infused sessions of The Big Sing, these events reinforce the global reach of improv and keep the art form evolving.
